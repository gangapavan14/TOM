## Problem Statement

Scale operators and mill staff face extreme turnaround pressure during peak morning truck queues (5+ trucks waiting at the factory gate). Existing ERP multi-step forms take >90 seconds per vehicle, prompting operators to bypass the digital system completely in favor of paper weighbridge slips and pocket notepads. 

These paper notes are manually transcribed into Tally/Excel late at night, allowing moisture deduction errors, weight shrinkage, unverified lots, and bag count discrepancies to enter financial ledgers undetected for days or slip through permanently.

---

## Demand Evidence

Observed operational facts from the active pilot:
- **Screen Bypass**: Operators abandoned the digital intake interface during morning vehicle rushes because multi-field web validation was too slow compared to paper scribbles.
- **Delayed Leak Detection**: Weight and bag discrepancies between the weighbridge gate and the godown stacks were caught days later during manual night-time Excel reconciliation.
- **High-Risk Capital Bleed**: Raw seed intake (groundnut/sunflower) accounts for >80% of total mill capital expenditure. Any discrepancy here creates compounding losses across processing yields and oil extraction balances.

---

## Status Quo

- Physical weighbridge paper slips and handwritten pocket tally books.
- Night-time manual data entry into Excel/Tally by tired staff after trucks have left.
- Unverified spot commitments on moisture deductions, tare adjustments, and bag counts.

---

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

- **Target User**: Weighbridge Scale Operator under high turnaround pressure, and Godown Senior Worker responsible for physical bag unloading counts.
- **Narrowest Wedge**: **Fast-Pass Intake Cockpit** — a single-screen, numpad-first terminal (<30 seconds turnaround) capturing vehicle number, gross/tare weights, supplier lot, and moisture %, auto-generating permanent location-independent Bag IDs, and holding stock in `PENDING_VERIFICATION` until Godown Senior Worker performs a single-action physical bag confirmation.

---

## Constraints & Invariant Rules

1. **Invariant Rule #1**: Stock is not inventory merely because somebody entered a receipt. Unverified stock must have ZERO available inventory balance and ZERO payable ledger credit.
2. **Invariant Rule #4**: Grade D condition is strictly eliminated; rejected stock never enters inventory or payables.
3. **Invariant Rule #5**: Permanent Bag IDs (e.g. `TUR-260926-001-001`) must never encode godown or room locations.
4. **Physical Speed Constraint**: Vehicle weigh-in must take under 30 seconds to prevent operator queue abandonment during morning rushes.
5. **Hardware Constraint**: Must run in standard browser on existing weighbridge cabin desktop without requiring proprietary hardware or barcode scanners on day 1.

---

## Premises

1. **High-Speed Gate Intake (<30s)**: We must prioritize a high-throughput, numpad-first gate inward screen before deploying complex processing, B2B sales, or payroll. If gate entry is slow, staff will abandon the system.
2. **Strict Quarantine Until Physical Count**: Inward gate entries must stay in `PENDING_VERIFICATION` with zero payables impact until physical bag counts and moisture grades are signed off (enforcing Invariant Rules #1 & #4).
3. **Dual-Role Verification Loop**: The weighbridge operator records the truck weight, but only the Godown Senior Worker's physical bag count releases stock into inventory.

## Recommended Approach: Fast-Pass Intake Cockpit (Approach A)

### 1. Numpad-First Single Screen UX
- **Input Flow**: Focus auto-advances across 4 inputs:
  1. `Vehicle No` (auto-suggests recent suppliers/transporters)
  2. `Supplier Lot ID` (or Quick Add)
  3. `Gross Weight (kg)` (direct numpad entry)
  4. `Moisture %` (instant calculation of net allowable weight)
- **Auto-Calculations**:
  - Net Weight = Gross - Estimated Tare
  - Expected Bag Count = Net Weight / Standard Bag Weight (e.g. 50kg)
  - Quality Grade: Automatically flagged (A, B, C; auto-reject if Grade D per Invariant Rule #4)
- **Fast-Action Hotkeys**:
  - `Enter`: Commit and issue gatepass (<15 seconds total transaction time).
  - `Ctrl+P`: Instant-print 2-part Gatepass Slip (1 for Driver, 1 for Godown Receiver).

### 2. Instant Quarantine & Permanent Bag ID Allocation
- Record created in `procurement_lots` with status `QUARANTINED_PENDING_UNLOAD`.
- Backend generates permanent location-independent Bag ID range:
  `TUR-{YYMMDD}-{LOT_ID}-{INDEX}` (strictly satisfying Invariant Rule #5).
- Stock is invisible to available production pool (`available_quantity = 0`).
- No payable entry generated in `supplier_payables` (strictly satisfying Invariant Rule #1).

### 3. Godown Senior Worker Physical Sign-off
- Godown portal displays a real-time queue of arrived vehicles awaiting unloading.
- Senior Worker taps the truck:
  - Enters actual counted bags unloaded (e.g. 210 expected vs 210 received).
  - Confirms no Grade D damaged bags.
  - Taps "Verify & Accept Stock".
- System transitions lot to `VERIFIED_IN_STOCK`, assigns godown storage bay, and simultaneously notifies Admin.

### 4. Admin Audit Cockpit
- Live inward discrepancy view: Gross vs Tare vs Physical Bag Count.
- Instant visual highlight if tare discrepancy exceeds 0.5% or bag count differs from estimate.
- One-click approval release for supplier payment release.
